SBEBA is holding a consultation camp at its **Kochi branch on Friday, 11 September 2026**. The
practitioners are Dr. Rajkumar K C and Dr. Remya Krishnan, both BAMS, MD, PhD.

The camp covers diabetes, allergy and hypersensitivity disorders, autoimmune conditions,
gynaecological and paediatric conditions, hypertension, and other chronic conditions, using an
Ayurvedic approach the organisers call the SBEBA protocol.

## Where and how to book

SBEBA, Kochi Branch, Opp. Axis Bank, Balan Menon Road, Kathrikadavu, Kaloor, Kochi 682017, Kerala.

Slots are limited and **prior appointment is required**. To book, call **+91 96339 00110**.

## Please note

This camp is being announced for the information of members. It is a private practice and is not
part of CHSS, and treatment there is not covered by the scheme.

**Do not stop or change any prescribed medicine on your own.** If you are considering treatment
here, talk to your existing doctor first, particularly if you are being treated for diabetes,
hypertension or an autoimmune condition.
