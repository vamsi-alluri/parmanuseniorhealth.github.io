Thermalytix is a breast cancer screening test developed by NIRAMAI Health Analytix. It uses a
thermal camera together with software analysis, rather than X-rays, to look for signs of abnormal
breast tissue.

## How the test works

The camera is placed about three feet away. There is **no radiation, no touch and no pain**, and the
person stays clothed and unobserved during the test, with the technician seated outside the room.
A test takes about fifteen minutes.

Because it does not depend on breast density, it can also be used for women under 40, for whom
mammography is not usually recommended.

## What the evidence says

The test has been studied in hospitals and community screening camps in India, with results
published in peer-reviewed journals including *The Lancet Oncology*, the *Journal of Clinical
Oncology Global Oncology* and *Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence*. In these studies it was used
**alongside** the standard tests, not in place of them, and an abnormal result was followed up with
ultrasound, mammography or biopsy to confirm the diagnosis.

## Please note

This is a screening test, which means it indicates whether further tests are needed. It is not a
diagnosis, and it does not replace mammography or a consultation with your doctor. Speak to your
doctor about which screening is right for you.
