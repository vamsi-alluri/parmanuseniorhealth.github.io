Ramakrishna Mission Hospital, Mumbai, is holding a **Holistic Wellness Workshop** on
**Saturday, 12 September 2026**. It covers lifestyle disorders such as obesity, joint pain,
sleeplessness, diabetes and hypertension, and is conducted by Dr. Swami Dayadhipananda, Medical
Superintendent of the hospital.

The session is **free of cost**, including registration, consultation and treatment. It will also be
streamed live.

## Where

OPD Room no. 10, Ramakrishna Mission Hospital, 12th Road, Khar (West), Mumbai 400052.

## Programme

- 10.00 am — Registration
- 10.15 am — Preliminary check-up
- 10.30 am to 12.30 pm — Consultation with Dr. Swamiji
- 12.45 pm — Light lunch at Vivekananda Auditorium
- 1.15 pm to 3.30 pm — Lecture, yoga practice, group discussion and questions
- Close of session

## Before you go

- Only people who have registered will be seen.
- Carry all your recent medical reports and the medicines you are currently taking.
- For details, write to holiwellmum@gmail.com.
